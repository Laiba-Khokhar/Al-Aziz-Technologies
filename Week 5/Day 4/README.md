# Day 4 – Data Storytelling & Professional Reporting

## 📊 Hospital Executive Performance Dashboard

### 📌 Project Overview
For Day 4, I worked on Data Storytelling and Professional Reporting using Power BI. I transformed hospital data into an executive-level dashboard that presents important KPIs, patient analysis, revenue trends, and operational information in a clear and interactive format.


### 📊 Key Skills Practiced
- KPI Selection
- Data Storytelling
- Choosing Appropriate Visualizations
- Dashboard Layout
- Visual Hierarchy
- Business Insights
- Data-Driven Recommendations
- Executive Reporting
- Time-Based Analysis
- Interactive Dashboard Design
- Power BI Service Concepts

### 💡 Business Insights
The dashboard helps analyze:
- Overall hospital revenue and patient performance
- Patient distribution across different diseases
- Patient distribution by gender
- Revenue trends and monthly growth
- Patient admission patterns
- Doctor and medicine-related information

### 🎯 Business Recommendations
- Monitor revenue trends to identify changes in hospital performance.
- Analyze disease-wise patient volumes for better resource planning.
- Monitor patient and billing trends together for better operational understanding.
- Review doctor and medicine data to support hospital resource management.
